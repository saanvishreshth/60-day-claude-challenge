Money Tips for Students

Give every rupee of your allowance a job the day it arrives — even "fun money" is a category, not an accident.
Try the 50/30/20 idea loosely: needs, wants, savings. As a student it's fine to lean heavier on savings if your needs are mostly covered.
Before buying something over ₹500, wait 48 hours. If you still want it, it's probably not an impulse.
Track subscriptions monthly — free trials that silently convert are the #1 leak in student budgets.
