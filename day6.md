## Key Learnings

1. ATS systems rely heavily on keywords.
2. Proper formatting improves readability.
3. Quantified achievements strengthen resumes.
4. Tailoring resumes increases job-match potential.
5. AI can help identify gaps and improve structure.

---

## Conclusion

The optimized resume is more ATS-friendly, keyword-rich, and professionally structured, improving its chances of passing automated screening systems.
