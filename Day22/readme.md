# Day 22 – Startup Validation Using AI

## Objective

The objective of Day 22 was to validate a startup idea using AI-powered market research and analysis. This activity focused on understanding the target market, evaluating the problem-solution fit, identifying competitors, assessing risks, and creating an actionable roadmap for startup growth.

## Tasks Performed

* Reviewed the provided startup validation resources and solution video.
* Used Claude AI to perform startup validation analysis.
* Answered discovery questions related to the startup idea honestly and in detail.
* Provided information about the startup, target customers, and existing validation.
* Analyzed Founder-Market Fit to understand alignment between the founder's experience and the problem being solved.
* Reviewed TAM (Total Addressable Market), SAM (Serviceable Available Market), and SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market) estimates.
* Studied competitor analysis and identified market opportunities.
* Examined customer personas and Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
* Evaluated potential risks and challenges.
* Reviewed the Go/No-Go recommendation provided by the AI analysis.
* Studied the 30-day action plan for validating and growing the startup.
* Saved the generated report and documented key learnings.

## Startup Idea

SkillBoost AI is an AI-powered learning and career guidance platform designed to help students identify skill gaps, follow personalized learning roadmaps, track progress, and improve placement readiness.

## Key Learnings

* Startup success begins with solving a real customer problem.
* Customer validation is more important than assumptions.
* Understanding competitors helps identify market gaps.
* A clear customer persona improves product positioning.
* Early feedback and iterative development reduce business risks.
* AI tools can significantly accelerate startup research and validation.

## Outcome

Successfully completed the startup validation process, gained insights into market opportunities and challenges, and developed a structured action plan for the next phase of product development and customer validation.
