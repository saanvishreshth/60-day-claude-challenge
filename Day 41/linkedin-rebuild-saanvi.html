Part 02 — the rebuild

Headline — 3 options

Option 1 — keyword-optimized
B.Tech CSE (Data Science) Student | Full-Stack Development, Data Analytics & UI/UX | Open to Internships
Use this if: you want maximum visibility in recruiter keyword searches. Every noun a recruiter might filter by is present.
Option 2 — value-proposition style
Building interfaces people enjoy and data pipelines that make sense of the mess | Data Science student seeking internships
Use this if: you're messaging recruiters or founders directly and want the headline to sound like a person, not a keyword string.
Option 3 — authority style
Data Science student — full-stack + AI/ML projects, 200+ DSA problems solved | ABES Engineering College
Use this if: you're posting content and want your headline to reinforce credibility on every comment and post you make.

Formula behind all three: [role/identity] | [what you do or who you help] | [proof or differentiator]. Pick option 1 for search visibility while job hunting — swap to 2 or 3 once you're actively networking or posting.

About section — rewrite

the hook (before "see more")

I build things that work — interfaces people enjoy using, and data pipelines that make sense of the mess behind them.

the story

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science (Data Science) student at ABES Engineering College, currently deepening my skills in full-stack development, UI/UX design, and cloud-based deployment (AWS, Azure, GCP). I care about where design meets data — interfaces that are usable, and systems that scale.

proof

200+ competitive programming problems solved (CodeChef rating: 500). Built a fully responsive e-commerce site from Figma prototype to production code. Collaborated end-to-end — ideation through implementation — on a Smart India Hackathon solution with a cross-functional team.

call to action

I'm looking for an internship where I can apply full-stack and data skills to real problems. Message me, or check out my work: github.com/saanvishreshth

Data ScienceFull-Stack DevelopmentUI/UX DesignCloud (AWS/Azure/GCP)AI/MLCompetitive ProgrammingInternship

Why this structure works: the hook drops the filler adjective and opens with what you actually deliver. The story keeps your real status (student, in progress) but frames it around interests, not a skills dump. Proof leads with your strongest number (200+ problems, 500 rating) instead of burying it. The CTA gives a reader an explicit next action instead of trailing off.

Part 04 — 7-day activation plan

Editing your profile gets you 20% of the result. Showing up in front of people gets you the rest.

day 1Make all profile changes
  • Replace headline with Option 1 (keyword-optimized)
  • Replace About section with the full rewrite above
  • Move projects into the Projects section with rewritten bullets
  • Update skills list: add the 10 recommended, remove the 2 flagged
  • Pin your top 3 skills
  • Add your GitHub link to the Contact Info and Featured section
day 2Publish post #1 — "I rebuilt my LinkedIn"

Post this to signal activity and invite engagement from your new, sharper profile.

I spent this week rewriting my LinkedIn profile from scratch. Old headline: "B.Tech CSE Student at ABES Engineering College" New headline: something that actually says what I do — full-stack development, data analytics, and UI/UX. Three things I learned: 1. Recruiters search keywords, not job titles 2. The first two lines of your About section are the only ones anyone reads 3. "Designed and developed" means nothing without a number attached Currently looking for a data science / full-stack internship. If you're hiring or know someone who is, I'd love to connect.
1,247 characters
day 3Send 10 strategic connection requests

Target: recruiters at companies hiring data science / full-stack interns, alumni from ABES Engineering College now working in tech, and engineers on teams you'd want to join.

Hi [Name], I'm a CS (Data Science) student building full-stack + data projects and actively looking for internships. Would love to connect and follow your work at [Company].
218 characters
day 4Comment on 5 posts in your target space

Use the value comment formula: Agree with a specific point, add insight from your own experience, then ask a question to invite a reply.

Example shape: "This matches what I found building [project] — [specific detail]. Curious how you'd handle [related question]?"

day 5Publish post #2 — a lessons-learned post
Something I got wrong on my first project (E-commerce site in HTML/CSS): I designed the Figma prototype after I'd already started coding the layout. Which meant redoing half the front-end once the UX didn't match what I'd built. Lesson: prototype first, code second. Obvious in hindsight — expensive to learn the hard way. What's a lesson you learned the hard way on a student project?
438 characters
day 6Engage with every comment + send 5 more requests
  • Reply to every comment on both posts within a few hours — this is what LinkedIn's algorithm rewards most
  • Send 5 more connection requests using the day-3 template, targeting a different company or team
day 7Review profile views and acceptance rate

Check your profile analytics (visible under your profile photo).

  • Profile views up sharply: your headline/keyword change is working — keep it as-is
  • Connection acceptance rate below ~30%: your request message is too generic — make it more specific to each person
  • Low post engagement: try asking a more specific question at the end, or post at a different time of day
