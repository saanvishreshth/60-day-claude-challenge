# Day 14 – Job Red Flag Detector

## Objective

The objective of this activity was to analyze a job opportunity using AI and identify potential risks, positive indicators, and overall suitability before applying.

## Tools Used

* Claude AI
* Job Description (JD)
* Company Information Sources
* GitHub

## Process Followed

1. Collected a Job Description.
2. Gathered company information.
3. Used the Job Red Flag Detector prompt.
4. Generated a risk analysis report.
5. Reviewed risk scores and recommendations.
6. Evaluated interview preparation questions.
7. Saved outputs and screenshots.

## Analysis Results

### Overall Risk Score

* Example: 35/100 (Low Risk)

### Red Flags Identified

* Limited salary transparency.
* Broad role responsibilities.
* Competitive hiring process.

### Positive Signals

* Strong company reputation.
* Growth opportunities.
* Positive employee feedback.

### Risk Breakdown

* Compensation Risk: Low
* Culture Risk: Medium
* Career Growth Risk: Low
* Stability Risk: Low

### Final Verdict

Apply with confidence after reviewing compensation details and role expectations.

## Key Learnings

* Learned how to evaluate job opportunities critically.
* Understood common employment red flags.
* Identified factors that indicate healthy workplace culture.
* Improved decision-making before applying to jobs.

## Outcome

Successfully analyzed a job opportunity and generated a detailed risk assessment report to support informed career decisions.
