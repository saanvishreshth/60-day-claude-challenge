# Day 3 - Role-Based Prompting

## Objective

Learn how assigning different roles changes AI responses.

## Question Used

How can I launch an AI-powered note-taking app?

---

## Prompt 1 - No Role

Prompt:
How can I launch an AI-powered note-taking app?

Observation:
The response was general and suitable for a broad audience.

---

## Prompt 2 - Founder Persona

Prompt:
Act as a startup founder.

How can I launch an AI-powered note-taking app?

Observation:
The response focused on business strategy, customer acquisition, validation, and monetization.

---

## Prompt 3 - Developer Persona

Prompt:
Act as a senior software developer.

How can I launch an AI-powered note-taking app?

Observation:
The response focused on architecture, APIs, databases, deployment, and implementation details.

---

## Comparison

| Aspect           | No Role | Founder  | Developer |
| ---------------- | ------- | -------- | --------- |
| Focus            | General | Business | Technical |
| Revenue Strategy | Low     | High     | Low       |
| Technical Detail | Medium  | Low      | High      |
| User Acquisition | Low     | High     | Medium    |

---

## Key Learnings

1. Role-based prompting significantly changes the perspective of the response.
2. Founder personas emphasize business outcomes.
3. Developer personas emphasize implementation and technical decisions.
4. Assigning a role produces more targeted and useful answers.
