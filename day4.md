# Day 4 – Chain-of-Thought Prompting

## Objective

Learn and apply Chain-of-Thought Prompting to generate a personalized career roadmap.

## Resource Used

https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/artificial-intelligence/what-is-chain-of-thought-prompting/

## Prompt Used
You are an Elite AI Career Strategist.

Your goal is to build a personalized roadmap for me.

Before creating the roadmap, ask me ONLY these 4 questions:

Question 1
What is your current situation?
Examples:
• Student
• Working Professional
• Freelancer
• Founder
• Career Switcher

Question 2
What skills do you currently have?
Examples:
• Python
• Marketing
• Sales
• Design
• Web Development
• Data Analysis

Question 3
What is your target goal?
Examples:
• Get a job
• Land an internship
• Become a Data Scientist
• Become an AI Engineer
• Start a business
• Grow on LinkedIn

Question 4
What is your target timeline?
Examples:
• 3 months
• 6 months
• 1 year
• 2 years

After collecting all answers:

Think step by step.

1. Analyze my current position.
2. Identify strengths.
3. Identify skill gaps.
4. Identify the fastest path to the goal.
5. Recommend learning priorities.
6. Recommend projects.
7. Recommend networking strategy.
8. Create milestones.

Finally generate a visually structured ONE-PAGE roadmap.

The roadmap must contain:

🚀 Current Position
🎯 Target Goal
📈 Skill Gap Analysis
🛠 Recommended Learning Plan
💼 Suggested Projects
🌐 Networking Strategy
📅 Monthly Milestones
⚡ Immediate Next Actions

PDF DESIGN REQUIREMENTS:

• A4 Portrait Layout
• Professional consulting-report style
• Clean sections with visual hierarchy
• Tables wherever appropriate
• No overlapping text
• Use concise content
• Use visual dividers
• Maximum one page
• Export-ready PDF format
• Easy to screenshot and share on LinkedIn

End with:

Generated using Chain-of-Thought Reasoning

## Questions Asked by Claude

1. What is your current education level?
2. What field or career are you interested in?
3. What skills do you currently have?
4. Where do you want to be professionally in the next 5 years?

## My Answers

1. Student
2. Become an AI Engineer
3. Web Development, Design, Data Analysis
4. I will see myself in a senior postion managing important portfolio of this company.


## Learnings

* Learned how Chain-of-Thought prompting improves reasoning.
* Learned how AI can create structured career plans.
* Learned the importance of breaking large goals into smaller actionable steps.

## Observations

* Responses were more detailed than normal prompts.
* The roadmap contained practical and actionable recommendations.
* Chain-of-Thought prompting produced clearer reasoning.
