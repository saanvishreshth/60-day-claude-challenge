WHY THE PARADOX
E85's lower energy density drops mileage to ~12.9 km/L (vs your 20 km/L), so the cheaper pump price is outweighed by burning far more fuel per km. E85 would only break even at ≤ ₹64.37/L — ₹17.63 below today's price.
