Control Tower Briefing

You're the Head of Operations for a global supply chain. Random operational alerts will appear in your feed — each with a countdown. Choose the right action before time runs out.

KPIs

  • Service Level — % of orders delivered on time
  • Customer Satisfaction — how happy your clients are
  • Inventory Health — stock accuracy & availability
  • Transportation Efficiency — fleet/logistics performance
  • Operating Cost — lower is better
  • Revenue Protected — $ saved from disruption

Tips

  • Match the action to the alert type — the "obvious" fix isn't always optimal.
  • Ignoring alerts is fast but risky — some fade on their own, most escalate.
  • Delaying a decision can help you gather more info, but time runs out.
  • As the clock winds down, alerts arrive faster and stack up. Prioritize Critical first.
