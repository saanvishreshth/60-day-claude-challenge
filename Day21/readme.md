# Day 21 – Digital Privacy Dashboard Analysis

## Overview

On Day 21, I explored the concept of digital privacy by generating and analyzing an interactive Digital Privacy Dashboard using AI. The dashboard simulated how personal data can be collected, tracked, and evaluated across online platforms. Through visualizations and privacy metrics, I gained insights into digital footprints, data exposure risks, and privacy improvement strategies.

## Activities Performed

* Used AI to generate a complete HTML-based Digital Privacy Dashboard.
* Reviewed the sample dataset provided in the prompt.
* Examined the Digital Footprint Score to understand overall online exposure.
* Analyzed the Privacy Score and factors affecting personal privacy.
* Explored the Exposure Heatmap to identify areas of high and low data exposure.
* Reviewed the Company Exposure Ranking to understand which organizations potentially collect the most user data.
* Studied the Digital Twin Profile to see how online behavior can be used to build detailed user profiles.
* Analyzed the Risk Radar to identify privacy and security vulnerabilities.
* Examined the Most Valuable Data Assets section to understand which personal data categories are most valuable.
* Tested the Privacy Improvement Simulator to observe how privacy-enhancing actions impact scores.
* Reviewed the Final Verdict summarizing overall privacy status and recommendations.
* Saved the generated HTML dashboard and tested its interactive features in a web browser.

## Key Learnings

* Every online activity contributes to a digital footprint.
* Organizations can build detailed profiles based on collected user data.
* Data exposure varies across platforms and services.
* Privacy scores can improve through proactive security and privacy measures.
* Visual dashboards help simplify complex privacy and risk information.
* Understanding digital privacy is essential for protecting personal information in today's connected world.

## Outcome

Successfully generated, analyzed, and tested a Digital Privacy Dashboard. The exercise provided practical insights into online privacy, data tracking, risk assessment, and strategies for improving digital security.
